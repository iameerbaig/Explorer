About Wanderlust

We're a small team of travelers, storytellers, and cultural explorers on a mission to help you find your next unforgettable destination.

Our Story

Wanderlust started as a shared spreadsheet of favorite beaches, temples, and cities passed between friends after every trip. It grew into a platform that curates travel recommendations across the globe, blending first-hand experience with a passion for cultural discovery. Today, we help travelers cut through the noise and find destinations that match what they're really looking for, whether that's a quiet beach, a centuries-old temple, or a country full of stories to uncover.
